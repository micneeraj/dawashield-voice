# User test results

One row per person who tried DawaShield. Record only what actually happened. Leave a field blank if you do not know it. Use a first name or an initial, never a full name or a phone number.

- **Age group:** for example 60+, 40 to 59, 18 to 39.
- **Language:** the language they used for the voice check (Hindi, English, Punjabi, Gujarati, Marathi, Kannada or Tamil).
- **Phone:** make and model, or Android / iPhone.
- **Finished by voice:** yes or no. Did they complete a check by speaking?
- **Tries:** how many attempts they needed.
- **Understood the result:** yes or no. Could they say in their own words whether the result was RED, AMBER or GREY, and what to do next?
