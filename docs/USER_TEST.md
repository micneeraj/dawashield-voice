# User test results

## Family voice test, 26 Nov 2026

- Testers: 4 family members, including at least one person aged 60+.
- Languages used: Hindi, English, Punjabi.
- Setup: each person used the live app on a real phone and spoke the medicine name and batch number from the strip.
- Result: all 4 completed the check by voice and understood the result.
- No participant typed anything; no developer assistance was given.
- Privacy: names, ages and phone details are not recorded, at the testers' request.
- Limits: this is a small, informal test with family members. It is not a formal usability study, and the observer was the builder.
